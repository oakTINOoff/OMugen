Play your biggest MUGEN collections from a USB drive or SD card on Android, plus a round
of fixes for every platform.

## Install

**Windows:** download **`OMugen-v0.1.4-windows.zip`** below, unzip it and copy `OMugen.exe`
into your MUGEN 1.1 game folder, next to `mugen.exe` (replace the old one if you have it).

> Windows may warn that the app is unrecognized, because the exe isn't signed yet:
> click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Android** (8 or newer, 64-bit phones with OpenGL ES 3.2):

> **Installed v0.1.3? Uninstall it first, once.** From this version on, the app is signed
> with OMugen's own permanent key, and Android doesn't update an app signed with another
> key. Your game folders aren't touched; only the app's settings (controls, frame rate)
> start over. Later versions install over this one as usual.

1. Download **`OMugen-v0.1.4-android.apk`** on your phone and open it. Android asks once to
   allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.
2. Put your MUGEN game folder (the one with `data`, `chars` and `stages`) in a folder named
   `OMugen` in your phone's internal storage, or leave it on a USB drive or SD card (below).
3. Open OMugen, tap **Allow file access**, then your game.

**Linux:** unpack **`OMugen-v0.1.4-linux.tar.gz`** into your game folder and keep the
`lib` folder next to `OMugen_Linux`.

## What's new

- **Games on USB drives and SD cards** (Android 11 or newer). Collections of 30, 100 GB
  don't fit on a phone: plug in the drive (a USB-C stick, or an adapter) and play them from
  there. Nothing is copied.
  - OMugen finds games in an `OMugen` folder on the drive, or at the top of the drive.
  - **Add a game folder** browses your phone and your drives: choose a game, or a folder
    of games, anywhere. Added folders stay in the list (✕ removes one; nothing is deleted).
  - The list updates when a drive is plugged in or out, and says where each game is.
  - The drive needs a format the phone reads: FAT32 or exFAT (most phones can't read
    NTFS). The game's `save` folder is written on the drive, as on a PC.
- On Android, **Back** on the game list closes the app, the frame rate setting only offers
  rates your screen has, and a finger sliding from a button onto the D-pad steers it.

## Fixes

- An error before the game's window opened (missing engine files, for example) closed
  OMugen with no message and no log. It now shows the error and writes the log.
- On Android, the error report no longer appears after a normal close for games with
  "Fatal" or "Panic" in their folder name, or because of an older crash; it only reports
  real errors. If the phone is too full to unpack OMugen's engine files, it says so.
- The engine files of older versions are cleaned up (about 20 MB each): on a phone right
  away, on a PC once unused for a month.
- Phone backups keep your pad layouts and the app's settings again (the engine files made
  the backup too big).
- The frame rate chosen in the Android app is no longer saved into the game's settings.
- On Android 8-10, after "Don't ask again" on the storage permission, **Allow file access**
  opens the app's permissions instead of doing nothing.
- A keyboard's Browser Back key (next to the arrows on some laptops) no longer acts as
  Escape on Windows and Linux.
- When a game's screenpack fails to load, the notice now shows the actual reason.

## Known issues

- Phones get warm in long sessions, older ones more. HD resolution (Options > Video) and
  60 fps run the coolest.
- Online and LAN play haven't been tested on Android yet.
- With some games in fullscreen on Windows, the NVIDIA overlay (Alt+Z) may not open and the
  mouse can stay frozen outside the game until you switch away. Being looked into.
