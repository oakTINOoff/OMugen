Online with a room code, characters added by dropping them in, and training tools.

## Install

**Windows:** download **`OMugen-v0.1.6-windows.zip`** below, unzip it and copy `OMugen.exe`
into your MUGEN 1.1 game folder, next to `mugen.exe` (replace the old one if you have it).

> Windows may warn that the app is unrecognized, because the exe isn't signed yet:
> click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Android** (8 or newer, 64-bit phones with OpenGL ES 3.2): download
**`OMugen-v0.1.6-android.apk`** on your phone and open it. It installs over v0.1.4 and later
and keeps your settings. If Play Protect warns about it, tap **More details**, then
**Install anyway** (see the FAQ).

**Linux:** unpack **`OMugen-v0.1.6-linux.tar.gz`** into your game folder and keep the
`lib` folder next to `OMugen_Linux`.

## What's new

- **Online with a room code.** No port forwarding, no server: **Host with Room Code** asks
  your router to open the way (UPnP, on by default on most home routers) and gives you a
  12-character code, already copied. Your friend picks **Join with Room Code** and pastes
  it. It's in ONLINE / LAN with the OMugen screenpack, and in **Options > Play Online**
  with any game's own screenpack. When your connection can't host (mobile data, two
  routers in a row, UPnP off), OMugen says so: let your friend host.
- **PC vs phone.** A PC and a phone can play the same game online: OMugen now computes the
  game the same way on both (some math used to differ between processors, even between
  two PCs). Both need this version and the same game.
- **Add characters and stages by dropping them in.** Drop a zip, rar or 7z, a folder or a
  `.def` file on the OMugen window (or on `OMugen.exe`), or pick one in **Options > Add
  Characters and Stages**; on Android, the phone's file picker opens. OMugen shows what it
  found and asks first, puts characters in `chars` and stages in `stages`, adds them to
  the select screen (in the next free box; `select.def` is backed up to `save/backups`) and
  restarts. Replaced characters are kept in `save/backups`; programs inside a download
  (`.exe`, `.bat`...) are left out.
- **Training Tools** in the training pause menu:
  - **Hitboxes**, without the debug mode.
  - **Input Display**: what each player holds, frame by frame, with button icons.
  - **Frame Data**: startup, active and recovery frames of each move, and the frame
    advantage after a hit or a block (+3, -5...).
  - **Record Dummy** and **Dummy Playback**: play the dummy with your controller, then
    let it repeat what you did, from either side.
- **Android 15**: the app now targets Android 15.

## Known issues

- Online between a PC and a phone is new: if a match stops with a desync message, please
  open an issue with both players' logs.
- Online play on Android hasn't been tested much yet; hosting from a phone needs Wi-Fi.
- Phones get warm in long sessions, older ones more. HD resolution (Options > Video) and
  60 fps run the coolest.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the downloads.
