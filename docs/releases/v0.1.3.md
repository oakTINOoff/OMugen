OMugen on Android, plus fixes for every platform.

## Install

**Windows:** download **`OMugen-v0.1.3-windows.zip`** below, unzip it and copy `OMugen.exe`
into your MUGEN 1.1 game folder, next to `mugen.exe` (replace the old one if you have it).

> Windows may warn that the app is unrecognized, because the exe isn't signed yet:
> click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Android** (8 or newer, 64-bit phones):

1. Download **`OMugen-v0.1.3-android.apk`** on your phone and open it. Android asks once to
   allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.
2. Copy your MUGEN game folder (the one with `data`, `chars` and `stages`) into a folder
   named `OMugen` in your phone's internal storage.
3. Open OMugen, tap **Allow file access**, then your game.

**Linux:** unpack **`OMugen-v0.1.3-linux.tar.gz`** into your game folder and keep the
`lib` folder next to `OMugen_Linux`.

## What's new

- **OMugen for Android.** The same engine plays your MUGEN game folders on a phone:
  - **On-screen DualShock 4** drawn over the game: several fingers at once, slide from
    button to button, a short vibration on each press. To the game it is a PlayStation
    pad, so your pad layout applies. Or choose **Bluetooth controllers only** and the
    screen stays clear.
  - **Frame rate:** 60 fps by default, to save battery and keep the phone cool, or up to
    your screen's own rate (90, 120, 144), and the screen is asked for the matching
    refresh rate. The game plays at its own speed either way.
  - The Back button works as Escape: the pause menu in a fight, back in menus.
  - Errors show up as messages on the phone. If a game closes on an error, the app
    shows what went wrong the next time it opens, saves it as
    `OMugen/OMugen-error-report.txt` and can share it. **Send the last game's log**
    shares the log of a game that misbehaved without closing.
- **The standard pad layout is OMugen's own** (the one its games are set up with): left
  stick and D-pad to move, A B C on Square Triangle Circle, X Y Z on Cross R1 R2, MUGEN's
  Start (energy charge in many games) on L1, and the pause menu on Options. Xbox pads get
  the same positions. It's used when a game's own layout doesn't fit your controller and
  you have no saved layout of your own; a saved one still comes first.
- **Portraits in the OMugen screenpack are cut like in the game's own.** Some games draw
  both players' portraits in one picture and let each side show its part. The OMugen
  screenpack showed the whole picture, so the fighter appeared twice. It now shows the
  same part as the game's screenpack, sized to fill the portrait box.
- **A missing font no longer replaces the game's screenpack.** Games often name a Windows
  font they don't ship, such as Arial. On a phone or on Linux, where it doesn't exist, the
  whole screenpack failed and OMugen's took over. OMugen's own font now stands in for it.
- When a game's screenpack does fail, the notice on the title screen says why.

## Known issues

- Phones get warm in long sessions, older ones more. HD resolution (Options > Video) and
  60 fps run the coolest.
- Online and LAN play haven't been tested on Android yet.
- With some games in fullscreen on Windows, the NVIDIA overlay (Alt+Z) may not open and the
  mouse can stay frozen outside the game until you switch away. Being looked into.
