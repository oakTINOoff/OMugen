A new display system: sharp on every screen, at any window size.

## Install

**Windows:** download **`OMugen-v0.1.5-windows.zip`** below, unzip it and copy `OMugen.exe`
into your MUGEN 1.1 game folder, next to `mugen.exe` (replace the old one if you have it).

> Windows may warn that the app is unrecognized, because the exe isn't signed yet:
> click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Android** (8 or newer, 64-bit phones with OpenGL ES 3.2): download
**`OMugen-v0.1.5-android.apk`** on your phone and open it. It installs over v0.1.4 and keeps
your settings. Coming from v0.1.3? Uninstall it first, once (v0.1.4 moved to OMugen's
permanent signing key).

**Linux:** unpack **`OMugen-v0.1.5-linux.tar.gz`** into your game folder and keep the
`lib` folder next to `OMugen_Linux`.

## What's new

- **Native resolution.** Until now every frame was drawn at the game's resolution
  (Options > Video > Resolution, often 852x480 or 1280x720) and stretched to the window,
  so menus, text and lifebars came out soft or, with nearest pixels at uneven sizes,
  jagged. OMugen now draws each frame at the size of the window's picture, in the screen's
  own pixels, and puts it there 1:1. The game's resolution still sets the picture's shape
  and the window's size. The frame follows the window: resize it, Alt+Enter, move it to
  another monitor. Online play, rollback and replays keep drawing at the game's resolution,
  as they must match on both sides. Options > Video > **Native Resolution** turns it off.
- **Better scaling when a frame isn't the window's size** (Keep Aspect Ratio off, a 4:3
  fight in a 16:9 picture, Native Resolution off, the Vulkan renderer aside): sharp
  bilinear with Window Scale Mode Nearest, so pixels stay even instead of jagged, and
  bicubic with Linear.
- **Sharp with Windows' display scaling.** Laptops usually scale the desktop to 125% or
  150%, and Windows then stretched OMugen's picture, so text and sprites looked soft, in
  fullscreen too. OMugen now uses the screen's own pixels. A window keeps the size it had
  (1280x720 on a 125% screen is 1600x900 real pixels), only sharper.
  `OMUGEN_DPI_UNAWARE=1` brings back the old behavior.
- **Windows that fit the screen.** A window too big for the screen (a 1920x1080 game on a
  laptop) hung off its edges, with the menus cut on the left and at the top. It now shrinks
  to the largest size of the same shape that fits, title bar and taskbar included, when it
  opens and when you leave fullscreen.
- **An FAQ** in the README for the usual questions: the Windows warning, where saves are,
  controllers, USB drives on Android, a game that crashes.

## Known issues

- Phones get warm in long sessions, older ones more. HD resolution (Options > Video) and
  60 fps run the coolest.
- Online and LAN play haven't been tested on Android yet.
- With some games in fullscreen on Windows, the NVIDIA overlay (Alt+Z) didn't open and the
  mouse stayed frozen outside the game. Seen with a window as big as the whole screen,
  which this version no longer makes: if you still see it, please open an issue.
